On 2026-11-05 a CPU run measured the synthesis language, built-in voice,
consented clone, voice-design, and transcription slice. That measurement is
not a pass. F104 stays unqualified.

Forty clips produced audio and none were silent. Nine built-in speakers ran
on their mapped languages. Ryan covered German, French, Russian, Portuguese,
Spanish, Italian, and auto. Ten x-vector clones used a synthetic Ryan prompt,
plus one English transcript-conditioned clone. Voice design covered the ten
named languages. The 0.6B custom-voice path forces instruct to None. Consent
was synthetic, with human_subject false.

whisper.cpp tiny transcribed the 37 non-word clips. Explicit language, auto,
and translate all returned. text, json, webvtt, and srt rendered. Diarize was
not run. The English sentence scored WER 0.0 for whisper.cpp tiny and for the
local faster-whisper small.en asset. OpenAI whisper weights and the Vosk
package were absent and were not installed. The timestamp reference matched
bravo and delta and missed alpha. Matched-word mean absolute error was 710 ms
at the start and 605 ms at the end for whisper.cpp, and 450 ms and 1180 ms
for faster-whisper. Those figures do not close the timestamp budget.

codec_language_id map. generate_custom_voice rejected both before synthesis
with ValueError, "Unsupported languages". Dylan and Eric remain Chinese
speaker ids. 1.7B Base and 1.7B CustomVoice were not fetched.

Still blocked after this measurement: dialect language arguments, 0.6B style
instructions, OpenAI whisper weights, the Vosk package, a verified
